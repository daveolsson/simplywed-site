# Piano Return Plan

Goal: play for fun and destress. Chopin nocturnes and Beethoven's Pathétique, plus Hans Zimmer and Ghibli pieces. 30 minutes a day on a digital piano. No pain.

Dashboard (private page): https://claude.ai/artifact/6yXqstnUrjYpugiauUbYqp
Local copy: `piano-return-dashboard.html` in this folder.

## Daily 30 minutes
- 0-5: warm-up (five-finger patterns, one scale and arpeggio)
- 5-10: a piece you already know
- 10-25: main piece, hard spots looped slowly, then one slow pass
- 25-30: play for pleasure, no fixing
- Twice a week, swap the last 5 minutes for the harmony task.

## Roadmap (about 12 weeks)
- Weeks 1-2: Chopin Preludes Op. 28 No. 4 and No. 6, Waltz in A minor B.150, Satie Gymnopedie No. 1
- Weeks 3-6: Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2; Pathetique 2nd movement (Adagio)
- Weeks 7-12: Nocturne Op. 72 No. 1 or C-sharp minor posthumous; Pathetique 3rd movement; then 1st movement last
- Later: Nocturnes Op. 27 No. 2, Op. 9 No. 1, Op. 55 No. 1

## Modern pieces
- Zimmer: Time, Cornfield Chase, Day One, Now We Are Free, He's a Pirate, Can You Feel the Love Tonight, Paul's Dream
- Ghibli: Summer, One Summer's Day, Merry-Go-Round of Life, Path of the Wind, A Town with an Ocean View, Carrying You, Always With Me
- Other: Einaudi Nuvole Bianche, Yiruma River Flows in You, Guaraldi Cast Your Fate to the Wind

## Harmony (10 min, twice a week)
Label chords in the pieces you play; I-IV-V and V7 in Op. 9 No. 2; the middle-section modulation in the Pathetique Adagio; ii7-V7-I in 3 keys; secondary dominants; diminished sevenths; I-V-vi-IV in 4 keys; the Neapolitan chord; borrowed chords in Ghibli; a 12-bar blues; improvise a left-hand nocturne pattern.

## Tips
Left hand first in nocturnes. Practice slowly. Loose wrist for tremolo octaves; stop at any tension. Use headphones and a real (half-pedal) sustain pedal. Record yourself weekly.

## Listening and analysis
- Mon: listen to a piece you are learning, no score. Wed: listen again with the score. Fri: analyze one excerpt in two or three lines. Sun: free listening.
- Five questions for any excerpt: form, harmony, texture, dynamics and pedal, your own choice.
- First excerpts: Pathetique 2nd mvt opening theme; Pathetique 1st mvt Grave and its return; Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 (returning theme, chords under the left hand); Zimmer Time (layers); Hisaishi Summer (reharmonized melody).
- Compare pianists: Rubinstein, Pires, Ashkenazy (nocturnes); Barenboim, Kempff, Gilels (Pathetique). Check what is available on your streaming service.
- Ear-training warm-ups (5 min): sing a bass line, pick out a melody by ear, name cadences, hum the melody over the left hand.
