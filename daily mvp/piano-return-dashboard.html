Listening and analysis

MonListen to a piece you're learning (no score) WedListen again with the score FriAnalyze one excerpt, in two or three lines SunFree listening outside your pieces
5 questions for any excerpt
  1. Form: where does the theme start, return and change?
  2. Harmony: first and last chords? Where is the tension and the relief?
  3. Texture: what does the left hand do, and how do the hands share the work?
  4. Dynamics and pedal: where are the peaks, and where does the sound open or thin?
  5. Your choice: what would you do differently, and why?

Compare pianists: for Chopin nocturnes try Rubinstein, Pires and Ashkenazy; for the Pathétique, Barenboim, Kempff and Gilels. Check what's available on your streaming service.
