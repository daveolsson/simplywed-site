The Story

Every couple deserves photos that feel as real as the moment itself.

SimplyWed Studio was built on a simple belief: wedding photography should capture who you actually are — not a posed, perfected version of your day.

Based in Vancouver, BC, we specialize in weddings and elopements that range from intimate ceremonies on mountain peaks to full receptions at the city's most beautiful venues. Whatever form your love story takes, our job is to document it honestly and beautifully.

We bring a calm, unobtrusive presence to every event — the kind where you forget the camera is there. The result is a collection of images that feel lived-in and true, not staged.

This is storytelling photography, fueled by passion.

What we believe
01

Candid over choreographed.

The best photos happen when people stop performing. We create the conditions for real moments to unfold.

02

Light over flash.

We work with available light whenever possible. It keeps things natural, beautiful, and true to how your day actually looked and felt.

03

Story over snapshots.

We're not collecting images — we're telling your story. Every photo connects to the next, building a narrative from start to finish.
